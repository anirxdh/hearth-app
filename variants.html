CHAPTER 6

Logo exploration · the mark

Your brief: a human heart in the mark, and you like the black-fire style of the current one. Here is the original plus six new options, including the phoenix you asked for, each shown at app, favicon and pin sizes.

1 · The Original Flame CURRENT

The hearth flame on an ember gradient tile. Warm, recognizable, already ours.

2 · Black Flame over Heart

Your black-fire style, hovering above a small open heart: the flame warms, the heart receives. Mono, sharp, prints anywhere.

3 · The RUA-Heart

A black heart with the flame carved out of its center in negative space. Two ideas, one silhouette: the strongest fusion of your brief.

4 · The Human Heart

A minimal line drawing of the anatomical heart with its vessels, carrying a gentle pulse line. Literal, medical, quietly beautiful.

5 · Heart of the Home

A roofline sheltering a heart: care that happens at home. Reads instantly at any size, and speaks the aging-in-place mission.

6 · The Ember Heart

One continuous shape: a heart whose top notch rises into a flame lick, in the ember gradient. The heart is burning warm, not burning out.

7 · The Phoenix Heart REQUESTED

A heart with a wing of three light strokes rising from it: care that renews itself, every day. Thin monoline, no heavy detail, very classy. The small size carries an ember-gradient version.

How to choose: option 3 is the strongest bold mark (heart + your black fire, one silhouette); option 7, the Phoenix Heart, is the classiest and lightest. Option 6 keeps today's warmth. Option 2 is the safest evolution of what exists. Tell us the number and it rolls out across the app, the site and the report.
